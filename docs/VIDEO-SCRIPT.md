# Five-minute demo-video script

**0:00–0:20 — Boundary.** Say that the displayed household and counterparty are synthetic and local. State that VERIS cites terms and does not decide claim outcomes.

**0:20–1:10 — Portfolio and cited answer.** Load the labelled synthetic household. Ask for the declared-condition waiting-period date and open the cited wording.

**1:10–1:50 — Honest uncertainty.** Ask an unsupported outcome question. Show the authorised-review state and the named document needed rather than a yes/no answer.

**1:50–2:35 — Protection map and illustration.** Show overlap, a neutral not-assessed item, and a cited health illustration. Say it is an illustration, not an amount payable.

**2:35–3:45 — Permissioned claim flow.** Prepare a synthetic claim, show its checklist and deadline, then show the exact payload confirmation and step-up boundary. Do not imply an external submission.

**3:45–4:30 — Settlement loop.** Show the local synthetic deduction review, dispute preparation, corrected synthetic receipt, and ledger verification.

**4:30–5:00 — Security and evidence.** Show hosted security posture, red-team report, scoreboard limits, and the evidence verifier. End with the human actions still required: hosting, reviewer study, recording, and submission.

Screen-record the running application after a human review. This script is not a completed recording.
